## Article 1 — Recognition
- **1.1** The Hospital recognizes the Association as the sole and exclusive bargaining representative for all full-time and part-time registered nurses employed at the Hospital, excluding supervisors, managers, and confidential employees as defined by the National Labor Relations Act.

## Article 12 — Staffing And Nurse-To-Patient Ratios

- **12.2** Workload Limits. No nurse shall be assigned patients beyond the ratios in Section 12.1. When census exceeds staffed capacity, the Hospital shall call in additional qualified staff before increasing any nurse's assignment.
- **12.3** Floating. Nurses shall float only to units in which they are competent and oriented. A nurse may not be floated more than once per shift.

## Article 13 — Staffing Committee
- **13.1** A joint Staffing Committee composed of equal numbers of Association-appointed nurses and Hospital-appointed representatives shall meet no less than monthly to review staffing plans, unit acuity, and compliance with Article 12.
- **13.2** The Committee shall have authority to recommend adjustments to the staffing plan. Unresolved disputes shall be subject to the grievance procedure in Article 20.

## Article 14 — Patient Safety And Assignment Despite Objection
- **14.1** Safe Assignment. A nurse who believes an assignment is unsafe may file an Assignment Despite Objection (ADO) form. Filing an ADO shall not subject the nurse to discipline or retaliation.
- **14.2** Safety Forum. A standing Nursing Practice and Safety Committee shall review all ADO submissions quarterly and report findings to the Chief Nursing Officer.
- **14.3** Occupational Health. The Hospital shall provide safe patient handling equipment and shall maintain a needlestick-injury prevention program consistent with OSHA standards.

## Article 20 — Grievance And Arbitration
- **20.1** Any dispute concerning the interpretation or application of this Agreement shall be processed through the following steps: (1) informal discussion with the immediate supervisor; (2) written grievance to the Nurse Manager; (3) appeal to the Chief Nursing Officer; (4) final and binding arbitration.
- **20.2** The arbitrator shall have no authority to add to, subtract from, or modify the terms of this Agreement.
